# Unsubscribe suppression scope and policy migration

Status: In progress
Owner: Neri Vale
Related: DOC-004; CHAT-004; DOC-005

## Description
This ticket began with workspace-wide unsubscribe suppression under D-07. Its current scope implements D-12: new unsubscribe events become publication-scoped after enablement. Existing workspace-wide opt-outs and hard-bounce suppressions remain workspace-wide. Deployment is not yet approved.

## Acceptance criteria
- Unsubscribing from one publication permits another only when no workspace-wide suppression exists.
- Existing workspace-wide unsubscribe records remain effective.
- Every scheduled campaign has a publication identifier.
- The preferences page offers a workspace-wide opt-out.
