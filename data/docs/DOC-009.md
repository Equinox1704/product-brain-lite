# Unsubscribe receipt copy and preference scope labels

Owner: Elian Marr. Scope: the confirmation displayed after a recipient changes campaign preferences. This document governs explanatory copy, not transport bounce classification or Pulse administrator onboarding messages.

## Current behavior
At this document's date, D-07 in DOC-004 defines unsubscribe suppression as workspace-wide. The confirmation must therefore say that campaign emails from the workspace stop, even when the recipient arrived through one audience's campaign. An audience name may provide context but must not imply audience-limited suppression.

## Decisions
D-21: We postponed publication-specific unsubscribe confirmation copy because the approved policy still applies unsubscribe suppression across the workspace. Showing narrower wording before a policy change would misrepresent the effect of the action.

D-22: We excluded promotional content from unsubscribe receipts because a confirmation should explain a completed preference change rather than pressure the recipient to return. The page may link to preference management without inserting a campaign offer.

## Acceptance and dependencies
The confirmation is available without signing into the sender's workspace. It must avoid exposing subscriber custom fields or the full subscriber profile. Repeated visits should explain the effective preference state without reversing it.

TCK-009 handles the copy and state assertions. DOC-008 governs hard bounces, which are delivery events rather than preference actions. If a replacement policy later introduces publication scope, a later-dated record must explicitly update D-21 and explain how old workspace-wide opt-outs remain represented. A design mockup alone cannot authorize the change.
