# Activation spec: onboarding email sequence

Owner: Mira Venn, Product. Scope: automated messages sent to new Pulse workspace administrators, not the in-app onboarding checklist.

## Problem and outcome
New administrators create a workspace but often leave before importing an audience. This specification proposes three lifecycle emails: a welcome message immediately after verification, an import guide after twenty-four hours without an import, and a campaign-building guide after the first successful import. These are Pulse product messages, not campaigns customers send to their own subscribers.

## Decisions
D-01: We chose behavior-triggered onboarding emails instead of a fixed daily sequence because completed actions should suppress irrelevant instructions. A completed import cancels the pending import guide.

D-02: We limited the sequence to verified workspace administrators because collaborators without setup permissions cannot complete the requested tasks. This is an initial scope restriction, not a deliverability requirement.

## Acceptance criteria
Each message contains one primary action, a preference-management link, and the workspace name. No subscriber addresses appear in these emails. An administrator who belongs to two workspaces receives independently evaluated messages, capped at one onboarding email per day across both workspaces.

An email delivery event does not mark an in-app checklist task complete. The checklist reads actual workspace actions and has its own acceptance rules in DOC-002.

## Measurement and open questions
Measure verified administrators reaching their first sent campaign within seven days. Track unsubscribe rate separately from activation so increased messaging cannot hide dissatisfaction. D-02 will be reviewed after collaborator interviews; no expansion is approved in this document. Delivery implementation and suppression tests belong to TCK-001.
