# October roadmap boundaries and release-evidence checklist

Owner: Mira Venn. Scope: consolidate the new workstreams without treating a decision record as evidence of release. This note complements CHAT-005 and preserves the unresolved states described there.

## Decisions
D-31: We require a linked verification record before changing a roadmap item to released because approved policy, implementation progress, and customer availability are different claims. A ticket marked ready for QA is not sufficient release evidence.

## Current boundaries
Subscriber CSV export remains postponed under D-05 and D-25. The field-permission harness in TCK-012 is a prerequisite, not a download feature. D-14's rejection of a support-operated CSV workaround remains binding, and D-16 still excludes rejected-row download files.

Aggregate campaign PDF reporting has no later release confirmation than TCK-004's ready-for-QA state. D-27 excludes automatically emailed report attachments. The in-app review-reminder proposal must not be advertised as a delivered PDF distribution system.

D-29 permits guarded manual subscriber refresh in policy, subject to its concurrency tests. It does not convert manual imports into continuous synchronization. D-30 permits rollout-gated publication copy, while TCK-005 still lacks rollout confirmation for the underlying unsubscribe migration.

## Review process
Each owner should attach a test result, the enabled scope, and the verification date before requesting a release-state change. A customer request can establish priority but cannot serve as that evidence. TCK-015 tracks this documentation gate rather than closing the underlying feature tickets. This record creates no new launch commitments and does not alter historical suppression or pricing decisions.
