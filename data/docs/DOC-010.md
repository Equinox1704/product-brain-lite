# Pricing preview specification for billable subscriber counts

Owner: Mira Venn. Scope: an informational pricing estimate for administrators evaluating a Pulse plan. This document does not create a payment system or promise a historical billing adjustment.

## Proposed presentation
Show the selected plan, the workspace's current eligible subscriber count, and the timestamp of the estimate. Clearly distinguish audience memberships from unique subscribers so one address in several audiences does not appear to multiply the estimate. Fictional pilot customers described this distinction as more important than a complicated calculator.

## Decisions
D-23: We chose a workspace-level deduplicated estimate because counting audience memberships would make the same reachable subscriber appear billable more than once. Suppressed subscribers are excluded from this proposed estimate.

D-24: We postponed annual-prepayment checkout because the team has not defined cancellation adjustments and renewal notices. A pricing preview can be evaluated without accepting money under an incomplete billing policy.

## Acceptance and boundaries
The page labels its output as an estimate, not an invoice. It explains its counting basis and avoids implying that it represents delivered messages. No subscriber-level list is downloadable from the preview, preserving the authorization boundary behind D-05.

TCK-010 implements the estimate display. TCK-011 records the rejected checkout request. Customer discussion in CHAT-009 should be interpreted as feedback on clarity, not acceptance of a paid contract. Changes to the counting basis require a separate decision and a visible explanation before they affect any future commercial agreement.
